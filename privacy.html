1. Introduction

X4 Wallpaper Studio ("Studio") is an independently developed tool that helps users create, modify, and prepare sleep-screen wallpaper for the XTEINK X4 Pro e-reader running CrossPoint.

X4 Wallpaper Studio is an independent project and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by XTEINK or CrossPoint.

Studio operates within the ChatGPT environment provided by OpenAI.

This Privacy Policy explains how information and artwork are handled when users interact with Studio.

2. Information Processed by Studio

Depending on how you use Studio, the following information may be processed:

  • Text instructions, descriptions, and other information you provide.
  • Photographs, illustrations, and other images you upload.
  • Images generated or modified during your session.
  • Artwork approvals, revision history, and related project information.
  • Finished wallpaper files created for your device.

Studio uses this information to provide the functionality you request.

You should avoid uploading sensitive personal information that isn't necessary for creating your wallpaper.

3. Information Collected by the Developer

The developer does not operate a separate website, user-account system, analytics service, or external database for collecting Studio users' information.

The developer does not intend to independently collect, access, sell, or share users' conversations, uploaded images, generated artwork, or personal information outside the ChatGPT environment.

Studio does not require users to provide their names, email addresses, or other identifying information directly to the developer.

This statement describes the developer's practices. It does not limit information OpenAI may process as the platform provider.

4. Processing Through OpenAI

Studio operates through ChatGPT and relies on capabilities provided by OpenAI, including conversational interaction, image processing, image generation, and file handling.

Information you provide may be processed and retained by OpenAI according to its applicable terms, privacy policies, and account settings.

For more information, consult:

OpenAI Privacy Policy

Studio's developer does not independently control OpenAI's processing, storage, or retention practices.

5. Artwork Storage and Recovery

Studio may create working copies of uploaded images, generated artwork, approved versions, and completed wallpaper files within its available execution environment.

It may also maintain records of artwork approvals and revisions to support the creative process.

The availability and persistence of these files depend on the capabilities and storage provided by the ChatGPT environment.

Studio does not guarantee permanent storage, indefinite retention, or recovery of artwork across conversations or sessions.

Users should download and retain copies of artwork and wallpaper files they wish to preserve.

6. Sharing and Third-Party Services

The developer does not independently sell users' information or provide their uploaded artwork to advertisers or external marketing services.

Studio relies on OpenAI's platform services to perform its functions.

The current Studio implementation does not intentionally transmit users' artwork or personal information to a separate developer-operated server.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Information processed through ChatGPT is subject to OpenAI's applicable retention policies and user controls.

Studio's local working files and project records may remain available for varying periods depending on the execution environment.

The developer does not operate an independent system for permanently archiving users' artwork or conversations.

Users should consult their ChatGPT settings and OpenAI's documentation for information about managing or deleting conversations and associated data.

Deleting a conversation does not necessarily guarantee immediate deletion of every copy or record maintained by the platform.

8. Children's Privacy

Studio is intended for users who are permitted to use ChatGPT under OpenAI's applicable age requirements.

Studio is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes in Studio's functionality, its operating environment, or applicable requirements.

The current version will be made available through Studio's public documentation.
